Cookie Policy

Last updated: 7 November 2026

The short version: dkvb.dev sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising trackers. It stores at most two small notes in your browser's local storage: your privacy choice, and (only if you accept) a reminder so the mailing-list pop-up doesn't keep reappearing.

1. Cookies and similar technologies

Cookies are small files a website saves in your browser. “Local storage” and “session storage” are similar browser features that let a site remember small pieces of information on your device. Unlike cookies, they are never sent to the website's server automatically. This policy covers all of them.

2. What this site stores

Nothing stored identifies you, and none of it is shared with anyone.

3. Third parties

No third-party scripts, embeds, fonts, analytics, maps, videos or social-media widgets load on this site, so no third party can set cookies through it. All fonts are hosted on dkvb.dev itself.

  • Formspree: when you submit a form, your browser sends the form data to Formspree. The site's request doesn't send or receive cookies.
  • Links to other sites: if you follow a link to GitHub, LinkedIn, Figma or a client's website, that site may set its own cookies under its own policy.
