My design process

A clear, collaborative process, so you always know what is happening and why.

  1. Discover

    Understanding your users, goals and constraints before a single pixel is placed.

  2. Wireframe

    Mapping out structure and flow, getting the bones right before the polish.

  3. Design

    High-fidelity screens with one visual language, a component system and full screen coverage.

  4. Prototype

    Interactive Figma prototypes for testing, feedback and sign-off before development.

  5. Build

    I implement the designs myself, so nothing is lost or misread in a handoff.
