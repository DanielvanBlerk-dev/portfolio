About me

I’m Daniel van Blerk, and dkvb.dev is just me.

I work from the Herbert River region in North Queensland. For the last two years I’ve been building Android apps, websites and the back ends that sit behind them.

Some of that work is live, like the Airlie Beach Art shop, which takes real card payments. Some of it lives on GitHub, like Skills United, a skill-swap app I built from start to finish. I’d rather show you what’s real than dress it up, so I’ve marked which is which.

Mostly I work with small businesses that need something built properly without paying agency prices. You deal with me from the first chat to launch, and after. If you’re local, I’m happy to come and see you.

I do use AI tools to speed up the build. Every piece still gets reviewed, tested and signed off by me.

Cheers, Daniel
